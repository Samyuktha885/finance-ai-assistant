# Business credit score / CIBIL rank (India)

Just as individuals have a personal credit score, businesses in India have a
credit assessment used by lenders when evaluating loan applications — commonly
referred to as a CIBIL Rank (for companies) or a business credit report.

## What affects a business's creditworthiness

- **Repayment history**: how consistently the business has repaid past loans and
  credit lines — the single biggest factor, same as personal credit scoring.
- **Credit utilization**: how much of available business credit lines are
  currently being used relative to the limit.
- **Financial statements**: lenders often review revenue, profitability, and
  cash flow statements as part of assessing a business loan application,
  especially for larger loan amounts.
- **Business vintage**: how long the business has been operating — newer
  businesses may face more scrutiny or need additional documentation/collateral.
- **Existing debt obligations**: how much the business already owes relative to
  its revenue or assets.

## Why this matters beyond loan approval

A stronger business credit profile can lead to:
- Higher loan approval likelihood
- Better (lower) interest rates offered
- Higher credit limits on business credit lines
- Faster approval processes with less documentation required

## General practices that support a healthy business credit profile

- Paying all business loan EMIs and credit line payments on time.
- Keeping credit utilization on business credit lines well below the limit.
- Maintaining clean, up-to-date financial records and statements.
- Avoiding taking on multiple new credit lines in a short period, which can
  signal financial strain to lenders.

This is general education on how business creditworthiness is typically
assessed — the specific scoring methodology and thresholds vary by lender and
credit bureau.
